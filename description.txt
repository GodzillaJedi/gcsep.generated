==============================================================================
                       COMMUNITY SOULS EXPANSION
==============================================================================
An ultimate cross-mod expansion addon for Fargo's Souls Mod. 
The core objective of this project is to bridge, scale, and balance mechanics 
across major content expansions seamlessly.

🌐 [url=https://terrariamods.wiki.gg/wiki/Community_Souls_Expansion]Official Wiki Engine[/url]
💬 [url=https://discord.gg/frUcz2dQAy]Join the Community Discord[/url]
🛠️ [url=https://github.com/Shtuks/StarlightsSouls]Open Source Codebase[/url]

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⚡ CURRENT FEATURES & OVERHAULS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Pinnacle Content Balancing — Integrates unique end-game Forces for Calamity Addons.
• Unified Playstyles — Merges Kinetic (SoA), Thrower (Thorium), and Rogue (Calamity) into a singular, balanced progression path.
• Unified Equipment Paths — Complete redesign and progression overhaul for Boots and Shields.
• Early-Game Soul Scaling — Expands 1st-tier Soul recipes and effects utilizing Thorium and Shadows of Abaddon materials.
• Captured Inhabitants — Adds functional caught Town NPCs for Shadows of Abaddon, Thorium, and Mod of Redemption.
• Expanded Mastery Trees — Adds fully tailored Enchantments, Forces, and Souls for Thorium, Shadows of Abaddon, Polarities, and Spooky Mod.
• Class Adaptations — Integrates unique Souls, Essences, and Mutagens for Clicker Class and Beekeeper Class.
